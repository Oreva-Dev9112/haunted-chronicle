# Credits

## 3D models
`public/models/kit.glb` combines models from these free packs, both released under CC0 (public domain). Credit isn't required, but they deserve it.

- **KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack** by Kay Lousberg: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-medieval-hexagon
- **KayKit Halloween Bits** by Kay Lousberg: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/halloween-bits

The models were merged into one file with gltf-transform. No other changes.

## Fonts
Caveat and Figtree, both under the SIL Open Font License, from Google Fonts (via Fontsource).

## Inspiration for the Chronicler's voice
Tone only, no lines copied: Bastion's reactive narrator (Supergiant Games), Darkest Dungeon's narrator (Red Hook Studios), Elan Ruskin's "AI-driven Dynamic Dialog" talk (GDC 2012), and Tracery by Kate Compton.
